# Illustratieprompts

Gemaakt met de ingebouwde imagegen-tool. De PNG-afbeeldingen zijn zonder bewerking ingesloten in illustraties.js zodat het spel lokaal werkt.

## Vrolijk

Use case: illustration-story. Create one polished children's picture-book illustration asset for a preschool dice game: Regenboog, the Rainbow Fish (de mooiste vis van de zee), HAPPY. A beautiful plump blue-turquoise fish facing right, whole body and tail visible, overlapping rainbow pastel scales and many luminous silver iridescent foil scales, lavender blue flowing fins, a large kind eye and an unmistakably cheerful smiling mouth. Hand-painted watercolor and colored pencil texture, charming expressive character, tasteful detailed storybook quality. Single fish isolated on genuinely transparent background, no scenery, no text, no border or watermark. Landscape 3:2 composition, fish fills frame with comfortable 8 percent padding, suitable displayed at 350 by 235 pixels. No other characters.

## Angstig

Use case: illustration-story. Edit the reference happy Rainbow Fish into an ANXIOUS version for the right side of a preschool dice game. Preserve exactly the same fish identity, watercolor pencil texture, rainbow and silver foil scales, blue lavender fins, right-facing orientation, full body composition, dimensions and background transparency. Change expression to clearly frightened/worried: wide open eye, eyebrow raised diagonally with inner end high, small downturned or O-shaped mouth, slightly tense fins. Gentle child-friendly apprehension, no horror, no tears needed. No text or extra characters. Genuinely transparent background.
